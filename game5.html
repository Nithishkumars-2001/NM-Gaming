How to Play Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors is the quick hand game, played against the computer. You choose a hand, the computer chooses too, and the winner of the round scores a point. This guide covers the rules and the scoreboard.

1. The goal

Win more rounds than the computer. Every round is one pick for you and one for the computer, and the scoreboard shows who is ahead.

2. Getting started

  1. There is no start button. The game is ready as soon as the page loads.
  2. Click or tap Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  3. The computer picks instantly, and both hands appear on the board.
  4. A message tells you who won the round, and the scores update.
  5. Pick again for the next round. You can play as many rounds as you like.

3. Controls

✊ Rock
Beats Scissors. Loses to Paper.
✋ Paper
Beats Rock. Loses to Scissors.
✌️ Scissors
Beats Paper. Loses to Rock.

Everything is played with the three buttons below the board. There are no keyboard controls.

4. How each round ends

  • You win. Your hand beats the computer's. The message says You Win This Round.
  • The computer wins. Its hand beats yours. The message says CPU Wins This Round.
  • Draw. Both picked the same hand. The message says It's a Draw and nobody scores.

There is no fixed end to the game. You decide when to stop, for example first to reach 5 wins.

5. Scoring and the round counter

Result You CPU Round
You win +1 – +1
CPU wins – +1 +1
Draw – – +1

The Round chip goes up after every pick, including draws, so it shows how many rounds you have played, not how many points were scored. Scores are not saved, so refresh the page to start again from 0.

6. Playing smart

  • The computer picks at random. Each hand has the same chance, so there is no hidden pattern to beat.
  • Don't trust streaks. Three Rocks in a row does not make Paper more likely next time.
  • Set a target. Agree on a first-to-5 or first-to-10 and turn it into a mini match.
  • Challenge a friend. Play on your own and keep your own score, or take turns to see who gets further ahead.
  • Mix it up. If you play a human, avoid repeating the same pick, because they will notice.
  • Enjoy the luck. This game is about quick fun, not strategy.

7. Common questions

Can I beat the computer with a pattern?No. It chooses randomly each round, so no pattern works in the long run.
How do I reset the score?Refresh the page. Scores and the round counter go back to 0.
Why did the Round number go up on a draw? The Round chip counts every round played, whether or not someone scored.
Is my score saved?No. Nothing is stored between visits.
Is there a winner at the end?The game never ends on its own. Whoever has the higher score when you stop is the winner.

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

A 3 × 3 grid of holes and 30 seconds on the clock. Tap the mole each time it pops up.

  1. Press Start. A mole appears in one hole at a time.
  2. Tap the hole with the mole to score 10 points. Tapping an empty hole costs nothing.
  3. The mole moves faster as your score climbs.
  4. When the 30 seconds are up, your score is compared with your Best.

Pro tip: Watch the whole grid, not one hole, so you can jump to the next mole as soon as it appears.

🎯 Guess the Number

A secret number from 1 to 100 is hidden. Use the Higher or Lower hints to find it in the fewest guesses.

  1. Type a number from 1 to 100 and press Guess, or press Enter.
  2. You get a hint: Higher or Lower.
  3. Use the hint to narrow down the range and guess again.
  4. Find the number to win. Fewer attempts means a better Best score.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of what is left. Starting at 50 finds any number in 7 guesses or fewer.

🔵 Simon Says

Four gold pads flash in a growing pattern. Watch, then repeat it from memory.

  1. Press Start and watch the pads flash. The Status chip says Watch.
  2. When it says Your Turn, tap the pads in the same order.
  3. Every correct round adds one more step to the pattern.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game. Best keeps the highest round you reached.

Pro tip: All four pads look the same, so remember positions (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right) instead of colours.

🔢 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger tile.

  1. Use the Arrow Keys, or swipe on a phone, to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, and your score goes up by the new number.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move that changes the board.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and avoid pressing the opposite direction.

Play 2048 →

🏓 Pong

The classic paddle duel against the CPU. Keep the ball in play and make the CPU miss.

  1. Press Start. The ball launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse or finger up and down over the board to control your paddle on the left.
  3. If the ball gets past the CPU paddle, you score. If it gets past yours, the CPU scores.
  4. The ball gets 5% faster horizontally every time it hits a paddle.

Pro tip: The CPU paddle never speeds up but the ball does, so long rallies work in your favour.

Play Pong →

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

Play Breakout →

NEXT UP 7 OF 7

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.
