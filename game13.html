How to Play Reaction Test

Reaction Test measures how fast you can react. A box turns gold after a random wait, and you tap as soon as you see it. This guide covers the rules and your results.

1. The goal

Tap the box as quickly as you can after it turns gold. You play five rounds, then the game shows your average reaction time.

2. Getting started

  1. The box says Tap to start. Click it, or press Space, to begin.
  2. It turns dark and says Wait for gold…. Don't tap yet.
  3. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW!.
  4. Tap or press Space as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown in the Last chip.
  5. Repeat for five rounds. After the fifth, the box shows your average.

3. Controls

Mouse or touch
Click or tap anywhere on the box.
Keyboard
Press the Space bar. This is usually faster than moving a mouse.
No buttons
Everything is done on the box itself.

4. How it ends

  • After five rounds. The box shows Average: … ms. Tap to start a new set of five.
  • If you tap too early. The box turns red and says Too early!. That attempt is cancelled, and your next tap starts a new set of five from round 1.

There is no way to lose points. You can try as many times as you like.

5. Times and best score

Chip What it shows
Last Your reaction time in milliseconds for the most recent round
Round How many of the 5 rounds you have finished
Best Your fastest single reaction ever, saved in this browser

The average at the end of five rounds is shown on the box but is not saved. Many people land between about 200 and 300 milliseconds, but it depends on your device and screen.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Don't guess the timing. The wait is random between 1 and 4 seconds, so there is no rhythm to learn.
  • Use the Space bar. Keep a finger resting on it. Pressing a key is faster than moving a mouse.
  • Stay relaxed. Tense muscles make you slower, not faster.
  • Focus on the colour. Look at the middle of the box and react to the change.
  • Reduce lag. A wired mouse or a fast phone screen can shave off a few milliseconds.
  • Take a break. If you keep tapping early, rest for a moment and start again.

7. Common questions

Why did it say Too early?You tapped before the box turned gold. The attempt is cancelled and the next tap starts a fresh set of five.
Does the first tap count as a round?No. The first tap only starts the round. It is not timed.
Where is my best time saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Is the average saved?No. Only your single fastest time is saved as Best.
Does the Space bar work?Yes. You can use Space for everything: starting, and reacting.

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.

Play Tic Tac Toe →

🃏 Memory Match

A 4 × 4 board hides 8 pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.

  1. Tap a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back after a moment, so remember where they were.
  4. Clear all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Every two flips count as one move.

Pro tip: Scan the board in a fixed order so you can recall positions by row and column.

Play Memory Match →

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Pick your hand against the computer and see who wins the round. The score keeps counting as long as you play.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a draw and nobody scores.
  4. Scores and the round counter keep running. Refresh the page to reset them.

Pro tip: The computer picks at random, so no pattern can beat it. Set a target, like first to 5, to make it a mini match.
