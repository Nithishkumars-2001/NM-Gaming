How to Play Memory Match

Memory Match is a card game that tests your memory. Sixteen cards hide eight pairs of symbols, and you flip two at a time to find the matches. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Find all 8 matching pairs using as few moves as possible. A move is one pair of flips, so fewer wasted flips means a better score.

2. Getting started

  1. The 16 cards start face down in a 4 × 4 grid.
  2. Tap a card to flip it and reveal its symbol.
  3. Tap a second card. If the symbols match, both cards stay face up and look dimmed.
  4. If they do not match, both cards flip back after a short moment. Remember what you saw.
  5. Keep going until the Matched chip shows 8/8.

3. Controls

Mouse or touch
Click or tap a card to flip it. There are no keyboard controls.
Wait for the flip-back
After a mismatch, taps are ignored for a moment while the cards turn over again.
New Game
Reshuffles all 16 cards and resets Moves and Matched.

Cards that are already face up or matched ignore taps, so you cannot flip the same card twice.

4. How the game ends

The game is finished when all eight pairs are matched. There is no timer and you cannot lose, so you can take your time to think. When you clear the board, your move count is compared with your Best score.

5. Moves, matches and best score

Chip What it shows
Moves Number of pairs of flips you have made. Two flips = 1 move
Matched How many pairs you have found, out of 8
Best Your lowest move count for a cleared board, saved in this browser

The best possible game takes 8 moves, which needs a lot of luck. Most players finish between 12 and 20.

Best only updates when you clear all 8 pairs. Pressing New Game early does not save an unfinished game.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Scan in a fixed order. Start in the top-left and flip cards in the same order each time.
  • Remember by position. Think "row 2, column 3" instead of trying to picture the whole board.
  • Use your first flip to explore. If you do not know any pair yet, flip a card you have not seen.
  • Use your second flip to check. When you know where a symbol is hiding, flip its partner straight away.
  • Group the symbols. The cards use spades, clubs, diamonds, hearts, a star, a moon, a sun and a flag, so make a small story out of them.
  • Don't rush. A quick wrong flip costs a whole move.

7. Common questions

Why can't I flip a third card?Only two cards can be face up at once. When they do not match, wait a moment for them to flip back.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Are the symbols the same every game?The same eight symbols are used every time, but their positions are reshuffled each game.
Does a match count as a move?Yes. Every pair of flips counts as one move, whether it matches or not.
What if I want to start over?Press New Game. Your Best score stays, but the unfinished game is not saved.

Memory tips

  • Start in a corner and flip cards in a fixed order, so you can recall positions.
  • Your first flip of a turn is a free look. Use it to learn new cards.
  • Wait for the mismatch to flip back before tapping again.
  • A perfect game takes 8 moves; most players land between 12 and 20.

How scoring works

  • Every pair of flips counts as one move.
  • The lower your move count, the better.
  • Your best result is saved in this browser.
  • Press New Game to reshuffle all 16 cards.

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Pick your hand against the computer and see who wins the round. The score keeps counting as long as you play.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a draw and nobody scores.
  4. Scores and the round counter keep running. Refresh the page to reset them.

Pro tip: The computer picks at random, so no pattern can beat it. Set a target, like first to 5, to make it a mini match.

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of holes for a split second. Hit them before they disappear.

  1. Press start and watch the holes on the board.
  2. Click or tap a mole as soon as it pops up.
  3. Each hit adds to your score, and missed moles earn nothing.
  4. When the round ends, your final score is shown.

Pro tip: Keep your eyes on the whole board, not one hole, so you spot the next mole early.

🔢 Guess the Number

The game hides a secret number. Use the hints to find it in as few guesses as you can.

  1. Type a number in the box and submit your guess.
  2. You get a hint: too high or too low.
  3. Use the hint to narrow the range and guess again.
  4. Find the secret number to win. Fewer attempts means a better result.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of the remaining range. This halves the options every time.

🎵 Simon Says

Watch the pattern of glowing pads, then repeat it exactly. It grows longer every round.

  1. Press start and watch the pads light up in order.
  2. Repeat the same order by clicking or tapping the pads.
  3. Get it right and the sequence gets one step longer.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game, so concentrate.

Pro tip: Say the sequence in your head as colours or a rhythm. Grouping it into small chunks helps.

Play Simon Says →

🧮 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger number.

  1. Slide all the tiles in one direction with the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into their sum.
  3. A new tile appears after every move, so the board fills up over time.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile before the board runs out of moves.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and build the other numbers next to it.

🏓 Pong

The original paddle duel. Bounce the ball back and make your opponent miss.

  1. Move your paddle up and down using the controls shown on the page.
  2. Bounce the ball back each time it comes to your side.
  3. If the ball gets past a paddle, the other side scores a point.
  4. Keep the rally going and outscore your opponent.

Pro tip: Move the paddle early, before the ball arrives. Hitting it with the edge changes the angle.

Play Pong →

🧱 Breakout

Clear the gold bricks with a bouncing orb. Keep it in play with your paddle.

  1. Move the paddle left and right to stay under the orb.
  2. The orb breaks a brick each time it hits one.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose it.
  4. Clear every brick to win the board.

Pro tip: Aim for the sides of the wall first. An orb that gets behind the bricks clears many on its own.
