How to Play Flappy Orb

Flappy Orb is a one-button arcade game. A golden orb falls under gravity, and each flap lifts it up. Fly through the gaps between the pillars. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Fly the orb through as many gaps between pillars as you can without touching anything. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start Game, or just click or tap the board. The orb begins falling right away.
  2. Flap to lift the orb. Each flap gives it a short upward kick, and gravity pulls it down again.
  3. Gold pillars scroll in from the right. Each one has a gap you need to fly through.
  4. Every pillar you pass adds 1 to your score.
  5. Keep going until you crash.

3. Controls

Keyboard
Press Space or the Up Arrow to flap.
Mouse
Click on the board to flap.
Touch screen
Tap the board to flap. The page won't scroll while you play.

4. How the game ends

The run ends when the orb touches a pillar, the floor, or the ceiling. The board shows GAME OVER. To play again, press Start Game, or simply flap. Any flap after a crash starts a new run.

5. Scoring

Chip What it shows
Score Pillars passed in the current run, 1 point each
Best Your highest score, saved in this browser when a run ends

The game does not get faster. Pillars always move at the same speed and every gap is the same size, but the height of each gap is random.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Use small taps. Short, regular taps keep the orb steady. Big bursts send it into the ceiling.
  • Aim for the middle of the gap. That leaves room for mistakes at both ends.
  • Look ahead. Watch the next pillar, not the orb. Start moving toward the gap early.
  • Find a rhythm. The pillars arrive at a steady pace, so a steady tap rhythm works well.
  • Don't panic after a tap. Give the orb a moment to rise before you tap again.
  • Stay away from the top. The ceiling counts as a crash, just like the floor.

7. Common questions

Why did the game start when I tapped the board? Any flap while the game is not running starts a new run.
Can I pause?No. Press Start Game to begin a new run.
Does the game get harder?The speed stays the same, but gaps appear at random heights, so some are harder to reach than others.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Why did I crash at the top of the screen? The ceiling and floor are solid, so touching either ends the run.

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.

🃏 Memory Match

A 4 × 4 board hides 8 pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.

  1. Tap a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back after a moment, so remember where they were.
  4. Clear all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Every two flips count as one move.

Pro tip: Scan the board in a fixed order so you can recall positions by row and column.

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Pick your hand against the computer and see who wins the round. The score keeps counting as long as you play.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a draw and nobody scores.
  4. Scores and the round counter keep running. Refresh the page to reset them.

Pro tip: The computer picks at random, so no pattern can beat it. Set a target, like first to 5, to make it a mini match.

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

A 3 × 3 grid of holes and 30 seconds on the clock. Tap the mole each time it pops up.

  1. Press Start. A mole appears in one hole at a time.
  2. Tap the hole with the mole to score 10 points. Tapping an empty hole costs nothing.
  3. The mole moves faster as your score climbs.
  4. When the 30 seconds are up, your score is compared with your Best.

Pro tip: Watch the whole grid, not one hole, so you can jump to the next mole as soon as it appears.

Play Whack-a-Mole →

🎯 Guess the Number

A secret number from 1 to 100 is hidden. Use the Higher or Lower hints to find it in the fewest guesses.

  1. Type a number from 1 to 100 and press Guess, or press Enter.
  2. You get a hint: Higher or Lower.
  3. Use the hint to narrow down the range and guess again.
  4. Find the number to win. Fewer attempts means a better Best score.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of what is left. Starting at 50 finds any number in 7 guesses or fewer.
