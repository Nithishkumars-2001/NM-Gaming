How to Play 2048

2048 is a sliding number puzzle on a 4 × 4 board. You slide tiles around and merge matching numbers into bigger ones. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Merge tiles to build the 2048 tile, and score as many points as you can along the way.

2. Getting started

  1. The game starts with two tiles on the board, each a 2 or a 4.
  2. Press an Arrow Key, or swipe on a phone. Every tile slides as far as it can in that direction.
  3. If two tiles with the same number touch while sliding, they merge into one tile with double the value.
  4. After every move that changes the board, a new tile appears in a random empty square. It is usually a 2 and sometimes a 4.
  5. Keep sliding and merging to build bigger numbers.

3. Controls

Keyboard
Arrow Keys: up, down, left, right. The page won't scroll while you play.
Touch screen
Swipe on the board. A swipe of about 30 pixels or more is counted.
New Game
Clears the board and your score, and deals two new tiles.

If a move would not change anything, nothing happens and no new tile is added.

4. How the game ends

The game has no timer. The board is stuck when every square is full and no two neighbouring tiles match. Press New Game to start again.

Note that this version does not show a pop-up when you reach 2048 or when no moves are left. You can keep merging past 2048 to push your score higher.

5. Scoring

When two tiles merge, you score the value of the new tile. Each tile can only merge once per move.

Merge Points scored
2 + 2 = 4 +4
4 + 4 = 8 +8
8 + 8 = 16 +16
64 + 64 = 128 +128
1024 + 1024 = 2048 +2048

Your Best score is updated while you play and is saved in this browser.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Pick a corner. Keep your biggest tile in one corner and never move it out.
  • Use only three directions. If your corner is bottom-right, use down, right and left. Use up only when you have to.
  • Keep a row full. A full bottom row of big numbers can't be disturbed.
  • Build in order. Arrange the numbers in a snake pattern, big to small, along the rows.
  • Merge small tiles early. Loose 2s and 4s fill the board fast.
  • Think before you swipe. There is no timer, so look at what each direction would do.

7. Common questions

Why did nothing happen when I pressed a key? The move would not have changed the board. Try another direction.
Does the game end at 2048?No. You can keep going and merge even higher tiles.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
What if the board is full?If no neighbouring tiles match, no move is possible. Press New Game.
Why do some new tiles show 4?About 1 in 10 new tiles starts as a 4 instead of a 2.

🏓 Pong

The classic paddle duel against the CPU. Keep the ball in play and make the CPU miss.

  1. Press Start. The ball launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse or finger up and down over the board to control your paddle on the left.
  3. If the ball gets past the CPU paddle, you score. If it gets past yours, the CPU scores.
  4. The ball gets 5% faster horizontally every time it hits a paddle.

Pro tip: The CPU paddle never speeds up but the ball does, so long rallies work in your favour.

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

Play Reaction Test →

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

Play Minesweeper →

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

Play Flappy Orb →

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.
