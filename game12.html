How to Play Hangman

Hangman is a word-guessing game. A hidden gaming word is shown as blanks, and you guess letters one at a time. This guide covers the rules and how to win more words.

1. The goal

Reveal the whole hidden word before you make 6 wrong guesses. Each word you finish adds one to your Wins.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start Game. A random word is chosen and shown as blanks, one for each letter.
  2. Pick a letter by clicking it on screen, or by pressing it on your keyboard.
  3. If the letter is in the word, it appears in every place it belongs.
  4. If it is not, you lose one life. The Lives chip counts down from 6.
  5. Keep guessing until you reveal the word, or run out of lives.

3. Controls

On-screen letters
Click or tap any letter from A to Z. Used letters turn off so you can't pick them twice.
Keyboard
Press any letter key on your keyboard to guess it.
Start Game / New Word
Picks a new word and resets your lives to 6.

4. How a round ends

  • You win. The last blank is filled. The message says You got it! and your Wins goes up by 1.
  • You lose. You make 6 wrong guesses. The message says Out of lives and shows the word.

After a round ends, the letters lock. Press New Word to play again.

5. Lives, wins and the words

Chip What it shows
Wins How many words you have completed in total, saved in this browser
Lives 6 at the start. Each wrong letter takes away 1

Only wrong guesses cost a life. A correct guess is free. Every word is a gaming term, between 5 and 11 letters long. The same word can come up again, because each word is picked at random.

Losing a round does not reset your Wins.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Start with vowels. E and A appear in many gaming words, so they are a safe first guess.
  • Count the blanks. The length of the word narrows down what it could be.
  • Think gaming. The words are about arcades, consoles, levels and scores.
  • Look for endings. If you reveal an R at the end, try E before it.
  • Use the pattern. When you have a few letters, picture the whole word before guessing again.
  • Save lives. Don't guess rare letters like Q or Z until the word forces you to.

7. Common questions

Why can't I click any letters?Press Start Game first to get a word. Letters also lock after a round ends.
Can I type on my keyboard?Yes. Press any letter from A to Z to guess it.
Where are my wins saved?In your browser's local storage. They are lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Will I get the same word twice in a row?It is possible, because words are picked at random.
Does losing reset my Wins?No. Wins only ever go up.

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

Play Snake →

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.

Play Sliding Puzzle →

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.

Play Tic Tac Toe →

🃏 Memory Match

A 4 × 4 board hides 8 pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.

  1. Tap a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back after a moment, so remember where they were.
  4. Clear all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Every two flips count as one move.

Pro tip: Scan the board in a fixed order so you can recall positions by row and column.
