How to Play Snake

Snake is a classic arcade game. You control a growing gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat orbs to score, grow longer and avoid crashing. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Score as many points as you can before the snake crashes. Each gold orb you eat adds 10 points and makes your tail one cell longer. The longer you get, the less space you have, so the game gets harder the better you play.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start Game. The snake begins with 3 cells in the middle-left of the board, moving to the right.
  2. A glowing gold orb appears at a random empty spot. It never appears on top of your snake.
  3. Steer the head toward the orb. When the head touches it, the orb disappears, your score goes up by 10 and a new orb appears.
  4. Keep going. The snake moves on its own and never stops, so you only decide when to turn.
  5. When the game ends, press Start Game again to play a new round.

3. Controls

Keyboard
Arrow Keys or W (up), A (left), S (down), D (right).
Touch screen
Swipe on the board in the direction you want to go. A short swipe of about 20 pixels is enough.
Pause
Press Pause to freeze the game and Resume to continue. Your score is kept.

You cannot reverse straight back into yourself. If you are moving right, pressing left does nothing. Turn up or down first, then turn again.

4. How the game ends

  • Hitting a wall. The edges of the board are solid. The snake does not wrap around to the other side.
  • Hitting your own tail. Any cell of your body is an obstacle, including the cells right behind the head.

When either happens, the board dims and shows GAME OVER. If your score beat your previous record, it is saved as your new Best score in this browser.

5. Scoring and speed levels

Every orb is worth 10 points. Each time your score reaches a multiple of 50, the speed level goes up by one and the snake moves faster. The top level is 8.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Think two moves ahead. Before you turn, check that the new direction will not trap you a few cells later.
  • Keep your tail away from the orb's side. Eating an orb tucked next to your own body is the most common way to crash.
  • Use the walls early. At low speed, travel along the edges and keep the centre open for later.
  • Make wide, slow turns. Tight spirals leave pockets you cannot escape from.
  • Use the queued-turn trick. You can press two directions quickly, but only the last one before the next step counts. Don't mash keys when you are near your tail.
  • Stay calm at higher levels. At level 5 and above, plan the route to the orb before you commit, and avoid last-second changes.
  • Pause to rest. Pausing is free, so use it to plan a tricky section.

7. Common questions

Why didn't my snake turn?You can't turn 180 degrees. Pressing the opposite direction is ignored to protect you from reversing into your own neck. Also, a turn only takes effect on the next step, so very fast taps may be skipped.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It stays after you close the page, but it is lost if you clear site data or switch to another browser or device.
Can I play on my phone?Yes. Swipe on the board to steer. Swipe in the direction you want to go, not toward the orb.
Does pausing cost me anything?No. Pause freezes the snake, and Resume carries on from the same spot with the same score and speed.
What if I want to start over mid-game? Press Restart. This resets your score and speed to the beginning. Your Best score only updates when a game ends, so restarting early does not save the unfinished score.

Strategy tips

  • Plan turns a few cells ahead; tight spirals trap you.
  • Hug the walls early, then work inward as your tail grows.
  • Speed rises every 50 points, so build safe space first.
  • Only one turn happens per step, so don't mash keys. The last key pressed before the next step wins.

Controls

  • Desktop: Arrow Keys or W A S D
  • Mobile: swipe on the board
  • Pause and Resume with the button below the board
  • Your best score is saved in this browser

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A scrambled grid with one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap until everything is back in order.

  1. Press the shuffle / start button to scramble the tiles.
  2. Click or tap a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides in.
  3. Keep sliding until the numbers (or picture pieces) are back in the correct order.
  4. Finish in fewer moves and less time to beat your own record.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the left column, and work inward. Don't undo finished rows.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

The classic 3 × 3 duel. Make three marks in a row before your opponent does.

  1. Pick a square to place your mark (X or O).
  2. Players take turns, one mark per turn.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. If all nine squares fill up with no line, the round is a draw.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first. Always check what your opponent can complete on the next turn.

🃏 Memory Match

Flip cards two at a time and find all the matching pairs from memory.

  1. Click a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Flip a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back, so remember where they were.
  4. Match every pair using as few flips as possible.

Pro tip: Work through one corner of the board at a time and say the positions in your head.

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

A quick best-of-rounds duel against the computer. Pick your hand and see who wins.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a tie.
  4. Keep winning rounds to build your score.

Pro tip: Don't repeat the same pick every time. Mix it up so the pattern can't be read.

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of holes for a split second. Hit them before they disappear.

  1. Press start and watch the holes on the board.
  2. Click or tap a mole as soon as it pops up.
  3. Each hit adds to your score, and missed moles earn nothing.
  4. When the timer ends, your final score is shown.

Pro tip: Keep your eyes on the whole board, not one hole, so you spot the next mole early.

🔢 Guess the Number

The game hides a secret number. Use the hints to find it in as few guesses as you can.

  1. Type a number in the box and submit your guess.
  2. You get a hint: too high or too low.
  3. Use the hint to narrow the range and guess again.
  4. Find the secret number to win. Fewer attempts means a better result.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of the remaining range. This halves the options every time.

🎵 Simon Says

Watch the pattern of glowing pads, then repeat it exactly. It grows longer every round.

  1. Press start and watch the pads light up in order.
  2. Repeat the same order by clicking or tapping the pads.
  3. Get it right and the sequence gets one step longer.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game, so concentrate.

Pro tip: Say the sequence in your head as colours or a rhythm. Chunking it into small groups helps.
