How to Play Guess the Number

Guess the Number is a logic game. The computer picks a secret number between 1 and 100, and you use Higher and Lower hints to find it. This guide covers the rules and the best strategy.

1. The goal

Find the secret number in as few attempts as possible. Your Best score is the lowest number of attempts you have needed to find a number.

2. Getting started

  1. When the page loads, the computer picks a secret number from 1 to 100.
  2. Type a number in the box and press Guess, or press Enter.
  3. A hint appears: Higher means the secret number is bigger, and Lower means it is smaller.
  4. Use the hint to guess again. The Attempts chip counts every guess.
  5. When you find it, the message says Correct! and shows the number.

3. Controls

Keyboard
Type your guess in the box and press Enter.
Touch or mouse
Tap the box, type a number, then press the Guess button.
New Number
Picks a new secret number and sets Attempts back to 0. Your Best stays.

4. How the game ends

The round ends when you guess the number. The input box and the Guess button lock, so press New Number to play again. There is no limit on attempts.

If you submit an empty box, 0, or a number outside 1 to 100, the game says Enter a number between 1 and 100. That does not count as an attempt.

5. Attempts, range and best score

Chip What it shows
Attempts How many valid guesses you have made in this round
Range Always shows 1-100, the numbers the secret can be
Best The fewest attempts you have used to find a number, saved in this browser

Best only updates when you find the number. If you press New Number before finding it, the round is not counted.

6. Playing like a pro

The trick is to always guess the middle of the numbers that are still possible. Every hint then removes about half of them.

  • Guess 50 first. If it says Higher, the number is between 51 and 100. If it says Lower, it is between 1 and 49.
  • Guess the middle again, for example 75 after a Higher, or 25 after a Lower.
  • Keep track of the highest 'Higher' guess and the lowest 'Lower' guess. The answer is always between them.
  • Never repeat a number. A repeat still counts as an attempt.
  • Using this method, you can find any number from 1 to 100 in 7 attempts or fewer.

7. Common questions

Does the Range chip change as I guess?No. It always shows 1-100 to remind you of the allowed numbers.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Does a wrong input count as an attempt?No. Only valid guesses from 1 to 100 are counted.
Is the secret number the same after I refresh? No. A new random number is chosen each time the page loads or you press New Number.
What is the lowest score possible?1 attempt, if you guess the number straight away. With good strategy, 7 is the most you will ever need.

🔵 Simon Says

Four gold pads flash in a growing pattern. Watch, then repeat it from memory.

  1. Press Start and watch the pads flash. The Status chip says Watch.
  2. When it says Your Turn, tap the pads in the same order.
  3. Every correct round adds one more step to the pattern.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game. Best keeps the highest round you reached.

Pro tip: All four pads look the same, so remember positions (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right) instead of colours.

🔢 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger tile.

  1. Use the Arrow Keys, or swipe on a phone, to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, and your score goes up by the new number.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move that changes the board.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and avoid pressing the opposite direction.

🏓 Pong

The classic paddle duel against the CPU. Keep the ball in play and make the CPU miss.

  1. Press Start. The ball launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse or finger up and down over the board to control your paddle on the left.
  3. If the ball gets past the CPU paddle, you score. If it gets past yours, the CPU scores.
  4. The ball gets 5% faster horizontally every time it hits a paddle.

Pro tip: The CPU paddle never speeds up but the ball does, so long rallies work in your favour.

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

Play Breakout →

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

Play Hangman →

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

Play Reaction Test →

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.
