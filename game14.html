💣 Minesweeper

Reveal every safe square on the 9x9 board and avoid the 10 mines.

Mines10
Time0
Best—

Click a square to reveal it. Numbers show how many mines touch that square. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on mobile, to mark a mine. Your first click is always safe.

How to Play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a logic puzzle. A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines, and the numbers on the squares you reveal tell you where they are. This guide covers the rules and strategies.

1. The goal

Reveal every square that does not contain a mine. There are 81 squares and 10 mines, so you need to open all 71 safe squares. Do it as fast as you can to set a new Best time.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start Game. The board stays locked until you do.
  2. Click any square. Your first click is always safe, and the squares around it are safe too, so a small area usually opens up.
  3. Numbers show how many mines touch that square, counting all 8 neighbours including diagonals.
  4. Empty squares open their neighbours automatically.
  5. Use the numbers to work out where the mines are, and mark them with flags.

3. Controls

Click or tap
Reveals a square. Flagged squares can't be revealed until you remove the flag.
Right-click
Places or removes a 🚩 flag on a square you think hides a mine.
Flag mode
On a phone, turn on Flag mode so taps place flags instead of revealing squares. Tap the button again to turn it off.

4. How the game ends

  • You win. All 71 safe squares are open. The message shows Cleared in … seconds!, and the timer stops.
  • You lose. You open a mine. It shows 💥, any unflagged mines show 💣, and the message says Boom! Press New Game to try again.

You do not need to flag every mine to win. Only opening all the safe squares counts.

5. Chips and numbers

Chip What it shows
Mines 10 minus the number of flags you have placed
Time Seconds since your first click. It starts when you reveal the first square
Best Your fastest win, in seconds, saved in this browser

If you place more than 10 flags, the Mines chip goes below zero. That is a sign some flags are wrong.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Start where the open area is. Your first click gives you a safe start. Work outward from its edges.
  • Match numbers to hidden squares. If a 1 touches only one unopened square, that square is a mine.
  • Flag as you go. Flags help you read nearby numbers.
  • Use the count. If a number already has all its flags, every other neighbour is safe. Open them.
  • Don't guess early. Look around the board for a certain move before you take a chance.
  • Watch the time. The clock only matters for your Best, so accuracy comes before speed.

7. Common questions

Why can't I click any squares?Press Start Game first. The board is locked until you do.
Is the first click really safe?Yes. Mines are placed after your first click and kept away from that square and its neighbours.
When does the timer start?On your first click, not when you press Start Game.
Where is my best time saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Why is the Mines chip below zero?You have placed more than 10 flags. Remove the ones you are unsure about.

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.

🃏 Memory Match

A 4 × 4 board hides 8 pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.

  1. Tap a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back after a moment, so remember where they were.
  4. Clear all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Every two flips count as one move.

Pro tip: Scan the board in a fixed order so you can recall positions by row and column.

Play Memory Match →

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Pick your hand against the computer and see who wins the round. The score keeps counting as long as you play.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a draw and nobody scores.
  4. Scores and the round counter keep running. Refresh the page to reset them.

Pro tip: The computer picks at random, so no pattern can beat it. Set a target, like first to 5, to make it a mini match.

Play Rock Paper Scissors →

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

A 3 × 3 grid of holes and 30 seconds on the clock. Tap the mole each time it pops up.

  1. Press Start. A mole appears in one hole at a time.
  2. Tap the hole with the mole to score 10 points. Tapping an empty hole costs nothing.
  3. The mole moves faster as your score climbs.
  4. When the 30 seconds are up, your score is compared with your Best.

Pro tip: Watch the whole grid, not one hole, so you can jump to the next mole as soon as it appears.
