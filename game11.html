🧱 Breakout

Bounce the orb, clear every gold brick, keep it in play.

Move the mouse, drag on mobile, or use the Arrow Keys / A and D to move the paddle. Hit the ball with the paddle's edge to change its angle.

How to Play Breakout

Breakout is a brick-smashing arcade game. You move a paddle to bounce a golden orb into a wall of bricks. This guide covers the controls, scoring and tips for clearing the board.

1. The goal

Break all 40 gold bricks (5 rows of 8) before you lose your three lives. Each brick scores 10 points, so a perfect clear is worth 400.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start Game. The orb launches upwards from near the middle of the board.
  2. Move your paddle along the bottom of the board to catch the orb.
  3. The orb breaks any brick it hits and bounces away. Your score goes up by 10.
  4. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life and a new orb is served.
  5. Keep going until every brick is gone.

3. Controls

Mouse
Move the pointer left and right over the board. The paddle follows.
Touch screen
Drag your finger across the board. The page won't scroll while you do.
Keyboard
Arrow Left and Arrow Right, or A and D. Each press moves the paddle one step.

4. How the game ends

  • You win. Every brick has been broken. The board shows YOU WIN.
  • You lose. You have used all 3 lives. The board shows GAME OVER.

In both cases your score is compared with your Best, and the button goes back to Start Game. Pressing Restart in the middle of a game does not save your unfinished score.

5. Scoring and lives

Chip What it shows
Score 10 points for every brick broken (maximum 400)
Best Your highest score at the end of a game, saved in this browser
Lives Starts at 3. Falls by 1 each time the orb drops past the paddle

The top two rows are a lighter gold than the others, but all bricks are worth the same.

When you lose a life, the bricks you already broke stay broken and your score is kept. Only the orb is served again.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Aim with the edges. Hitting the orb with the left side of the paddle sends it left, and the right side sends it right. The centre sends it more or less straight up.
  • Break a path to the top. Clearing one side column lets the orb get behind the wall, where it can break many bricks on its own.
  • Keep your eyes on the orb. Your paddle follows your hand automatically, so watch where the orb is going.
  • Get under it early. Move the paddle before the orb arrives, not as it arrives.
  • Be ready after a miss. The new orb starts in the middle of the board, moving sideways and up.
  • Use small keyboard taps. Tapping Arrow keys gives finer control than holding them.

7. Common questions

Why does the orb go sideways?Where the orb hits the paddle decides its sideways speed. Hits near the edges give a sharper angle.
Do I start over when I lose a life?No. Your score and broken bricks stay. Only the orb restarts.
Is there a pause button?No. Press Restart to start a new game.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Can I play on my phone?Yes. Drag your finger across the board to move the paddle.

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

Play Snake →

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.
