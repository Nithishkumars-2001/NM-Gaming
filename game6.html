How to Play Whack-a-Mole

Whack-a-Mole is a 30-second reflex game. A mole pops up in one of nine holes and you tap it before it moves on. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Score as many points as you can in 30 seconds. Every mole you hit is worth 10 points, and the faster and more accurate you are, the higher you climb.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start. The 3 × 3 grid of holes is built and the 30-second timer begins.
  2. A mole 🐹 appears in one hole. Only one mole is shown at a time.
  3. Tap that hole. A 💥 flashes, and your score goes up by 10.
  4. The mole keeps jumping to new holes on its own, whether you hit it or not.
  5. When the Time chip reaches 0, the round is over.

3. Controls

Mouse or touch
Click or tap the hole where the mole is. There are no keyboard controls.
Start / Restart
Starts a new 30-second round. Pressing Restart in the middle of a round resets your score and the timer.
No penalty for misses
Tapping an empty hole does nothing and does not cost points.

4. How the game ends

The round ends when the timer reaches 0. The mole disappears and the board clears. There are no lives, so you cannot lose early.

When the round ends, your score is compared with your Best. If it is higher, it becomes the new Best and is saved in this browser. Restarting early does not save the unfinished score.

5. Scoring and speed

Each hit is worth 10 points. The mole also gets quicker as your score grows, because it stays in each hole for a shorter time.

Your score Mole stays for about
0 pts 650 ms
10 pts 570 ms
20 pts 490 ms
30 pts 410 ms
40 pts 330 ms
50 pts and up 280 ms (top speed)

Top speed is reached after only 5 hits, so the last part of the round is always at full pace.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Look at the whole grid. Don't stare at one hole. Let your eyes cover all nine.
  • Be ready to move. The mole can appear in any hole next, including the one you just used.
  • Keep your hand light. Hover over the middle of the grid so every hole is a short move.
  • Don't chase misses. If you were late, forget it and focus on the next mole.
  • Stay calm after 50 points. The speed stops increasing, so you can settle into a steady rhythm.
  • Use both thumbs on a phone. It halves the distance you have to travel.

7. Common questions

Do I lose points if I miss?No. Tapping an empty hole has no effect on your score.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Can I pause the game?There is no pause button. Press Restart to start a new round.
Why does the mole get faster?The time it stays in a hole shrinks as your score grows, down to a minimum of 280 ms.
Does the best score update if I restart? No. Best only updates when the 30 seconds run out.

🎯 Guess the Number

A secret number from 1 to 100 is hidden. Use the Higher or Lower hints to find it in the fewest guesses.

  1. Type a number from 1 to 100 and press Guess, or press Enter.
  2. You get a hint: Higher or Lower.
  3. Use the hint to narrow down the range and guess again.
  4. Find the number to win. Fewer attempts means a better Best score.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of what is left. Starting at 50 finds any number in 7 guesses or fewer.

🔵 Simon Says

Four gold pads flash in a growing pattern. Watch, then repeat it from memory.

  1. Press Start and watch the pads flash. The Status chip says Watch.
  2. When it says Your Turn, tap the pads in the same order.
  3. Every correct round adds one more step to the pattern.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game. Best keeps the highest round you reached.

Pro tip: All four pads look the same, so remember positions (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right) instead of colours.

🔢 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger tile.

  1. Use the Arrow Keys, or swipe on a phone, to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, and your score goes up by the new number.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move that changes the board.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and avoid pressing the opposite direction.

🏓 Pong

The classic paddle duel against the CPU. Keep the ball in play and make the CPU miss.

  1. Press Start. The ball launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse or finger up and down over the board to control your paddle on the left.
  3. If the ball gets past the CPU paddle, you score. If it gets past yours, the CPU scores.
  4. The ball gets 5% faster horizontally every time it hits a paddle.

Pro tip: The CPU paddle never speeds up but the ball does, so long rallies work in your favour.

Play Pong →

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

Play Breakout →

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

Play Hangman →

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.
