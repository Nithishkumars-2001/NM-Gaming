How to Play Pong

Pong is the original arcade duel. You control the left paddle, the CPU controls the right, and the ball bounces between you. This guide covers the rules and how to win more rallies.

1. The goal

Score more points than the CPU by letting the ball get past its paddle. There is no winning score, so you decide when you have had enough.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start. The ball begins in the centre and launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse, or slide your finger, up and down over the board. Your paddle on the left follows.
  3. Bounce the ball back each time it comes to your side.
  4. If the ball passes the CPU's paddle on the right, you score a point.
  5. If the ball passes your paddle, the CPU scores a point, and the ball restarts from the centre.

3. Controls

Mouse
Move the pointer up and down over the board. The paddle follows its height.
Touch screen
Drag your finger up and down on the board. The page won't scroll while you do.
Start
Starts the game and sets both scores to 0. It works once per page load.

There are no keyboard controls in Pong.

4. How the game ends

Pong keeps running until you leave the page. The ball keeps being served after every point.

Once the game is running, pressing Start does nothing. To reset the scores and play again from 0, refresh the page.

5. Scoring and ball speed

What happens Result
Ball passes the CPU paddle You +1
Ball passes your paddle CPU +1
After every point Ball returns to the centre at starting speed

Every time the ball hits a paddle, yours or the CPU's, it gets 5% faster across the board. Here is how the horizontal speed grows during a rally:

Paddle hits in a rally Ball speed (pixels per frame)
0 4.0
5 5.1
10 6.5
15 8.3
20 10.6

6. Playing like a pro

  • Move early. Start moving your paddle as soon as the ball leaves the CPU's side.
  • Stay near the centre. Between points, return to the middle so you can reach both corners.
  • Long rallies favour you. The ball speeds up with every hit, but the CPU paddle always moves at the same speed.
  • Watch the ball, not the paddle. Your paddle follows your hand automatically.
  • Use smooth movements. Sudden jumps can make you overshoot.
  • Keep your hand steady. On a phone, rest your wrist so your finger doesn't wander.

7. Common questions

How do I restart?Refresh the page. The Start button only works when no game is running.
Is my score saved?No. Scores last only until you refresh or leave the page.
Can I use the keyboard?No. Pong is controlled with the mouse or touch only.
Does hitting the ball with the edge of the paddle change its angle?No. The ball bounces straight back with the same vertical speed, so timing and position matter more than angle.
Why does the CPU keep catching the ball? Its paddle follows the ball at a steady speed. Win by making the ball fast enough in a long rally that the CPU cannot keep up.

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.

Play Flappy Orb →

🐍 Snake

A gold snake on a 20 × 20 board. Eat the glowing orbs to grow without crashing.

  1. Press Start Game. The snake moves on its own.
  2. Steer with the Arrow Keys or W A S D, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Every orb you eat scores 10 points and makes the snake longer. The speed goes up every 50 points.
  4. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Pro tip: Plan turns a few cells ahead. Tight spirals trap you.

Play Snake →

🧩 Sliding Puzzle

A 3 × 3 board with tiles 1 to 8 and one empty slot. Slide tiles into the gap to put them in order.

  1. The board starts shuffled. Press Shuffle any time for a fresh scramble.
  2. Click a tile that touches the empty slot and it slides into the gap.
  3. Arrange tiles 1 to 8 in reading order, left to right and top to bottom.
  4. Solve it in fewer moves to set a new best.

Pro tip: Fix the top row first, then the middle row, and leave the last two rows for the end.
