How to Play Sliding Puzzle

Sliding Puzzle is a classic tile game on a 3 × 3 board. You slide numbered tiles around the one empty slot until they are back in order. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Put the tiles 1 to 8 in reading order: 1, 2, 3 on the top row, 4, 5, 6 in the middle and 7, 8 on the bottom, with the empty slot in the bottom-right corner. Try to do it in as few moves as possible.

2. Getting started

  1. When the page loads, the board is already shuffled and the status chip shows In Progress.
  2. Look for the empty slot. Only tiles that touch it can move.
  3. Click or tap a tile next to the gap. It slides into the empty slot, and the gap moves to where the tile was.
  4. Keep sliding tiles, one at a time, until all eight are in order.
  5. Press Shuffle when you want a new puzzle.

3. Controls

Mouse or touch
Click or tap the tile you want to slide. There are no keyboard controls.
Next to the gap only
A tile moves only if it shares a side with the empty slot. Tiles that touch only at a corner do not count.
Shuffle
Scrambles the board and sets Moves back to 0. Use it to start over at any time.

4. How the game ends

The puzzle is solved when tiles 1 to 8 are in order and the empty slot is last. The status chip changes to Solved! 🎉. There is no timer and no way to lose, so you can take as long as you like.

Every shuffled board can be solved. The game only creates boards that have a solution, so if you feel stuck, the puzzle is still solvable.

5. Moves, best score and the status chip

Each tile you slide counts as one move. Your Best score is the lowest number of moves you have used to solve a puzzle, and it is saved in this browser.

Chip What it shows
Moves How many tiles you have slid on the current puzzle
Best Your lowest move count for a solved puzzle (– if you have not solved one yet)
Status In Progress while you play, Solved! 🎉 when the tiles are in order

6. Solving like a pro

  • Work row by row. Solve the top row (1, 2, 3) first, then do not touch it again.
  • Then the middle row. Once the top row is done, you are left with a smaller 2 × 3 puzzle.
  • Think about the gap. You are not really moving tiles, you are moving the empty slot around them.
  • Avoid random sliding. Moving tiles back and forth only adds to your move count.
  • Plan before you click. There is no timer, so look two or three moves ahead.
  • Don't break finished tiles. If you must move one, plan how to put it back.

7. Common questions

Why won't a tile move?Only tiles that share a side with the empty slot can slide. Click a tile that is directly above, below, left or right of the gap.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It stays after you close the page, but it is lost if you clear site data or use another browser or device.
Does Shuffle cost me anything?It resets Moves to 0 and gives you a new puzzle. Your Best score stays, but an unfinished puzzle does not count.
Is there an undo button?No. To go back, slide the tile in the opposite direction.
Can I get a puzzle that is impossible?No. The game checks every shuffle and only gives you boards that can be solved.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Two players share one screen. Line up three of your marks before the other player does.

  1. X always goes first. Tap an empty square to place your mark.
  2. The turn passes automatically between X and O.
  3. Three in a row (across, down or diagonal) wins the round.
  4. The scoreboard counts X wins and O wins. Press New Round for a fresh board.

Pro tip: Take the centre or a corner first, and always check what the other player can complete next turn.

🃏 Memory Match

A 4 × 4 board hides 8 pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.

  1. Tap a card to flip it and see the symbol.
  2. Tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back after a moment, so remember where they were.
  4. Clear all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Every two flips count as one move.

Pro tip: Scan the board in a fixed order so you can recall positions by row and column.

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Pick your hand against the computer and see who wins the round. The score keeps counting as long as you play.

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both pick the same, the round is a draw and nobody scores.
  4. Scores and the round counter keep running. Refresh the page to reset them.

Pro tip: The computer picks at random, so no pattern can beat it. Set a target, like first to 5, to make it a mini match.

🔨 Whack-a-Mole

A 3 × 3 grid of holes and 30 seconds on the clock. Tap the mole each time it pops up.

  1. Press Start. A mole appears in one hole at a time.
  2. Tap the hole with the mole to score 10 points. Tapping an empty hole costs nothing.
  3. The mole moves faster as your score climbs.
  4. When the 30 seconds are up, your score is compared with your Best.

Pro tip: Watch the whole grid, not one hole, so you can jump to the next mole as soon as it appears.

🎯 Guess the Number

A secret number from 1 to 100 is hidden. Use the Higher or Lower hints to find it in the fewest guesses.

  1. Type a number from 1 to 100 and press Guess, or press Enter.
  2. You get a hint: Higher or Lower.
  3. Use the hint to narrow down the range and guess again.
  4. Find the number to win. Fewer attempts means a better Best score.

Pro tip: Always guess the middle of what is left. Starting at 50 finds any number in 7 guesses or fewer.

🔵 Simon Says

Four gold pads flash in a growing pattern. Watch, then repeat it from memory.

  1. Press Start and watch the pads flash. The Status chip says Watch.
  2. When it says Your Turn, tap the pads in the same order.
  3. Every correct round adds one more step to the pattern.
  4. One wrong tap ends the game. Best keeps the highest round you reached.

Pro tip: All four pads look the same, so remember positions (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right) instead of colours.

Play Simon Says →

🔢 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger tile.

  1. Use the Arrow Keys, or swipe on a phone, to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, and your score goes up by the new number.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move that changes the board.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and avoid pressing the opposite direction.
