How to Play Simon Says

Simon Says is a memory game. Four pads flash in a pattern, and you repeat it back. Each round adds one more step. This guide covers everything you need to know.

1. The goal

Repeat the pattern correctly for as many rounds as you can. Your Best score is the highest round you have reached.

2. Getting started

  1. Press Start. The Round chip shows 1 and the Status chip changes to Watch.
  2. The pads light up in light gold, one at a time. In round 1 you see one flash.
  3. When the Status chip says Your Turn, tap the pads in the same order.
  4. If you get it right, a new round starts. The same pattern plays again with one new step added at the end.
  5. Keep going for as long as you can remember the pattern.

3. Controls

Mouse or touch
Tap the pads. The four pads sit in a 2 × 2 square.
Start / Restart / Try Again
Starts a new game with a brand new pattern from round 1.
Wait for Your Turn
Taps are ignored while the pattern is playing. Wait for the Status chip to change.

4. How the game ends

One wrong tap ends the game. The Status chip changes to Game Over and the button changes to Try Again. There is no timer, so you can take as long as you like to remember each step.

5. Rounds, best score and status

Chip What it shows
Round The round you are on. It is also the number of steps in the pattern
Best The highest round you have reached, saved in this browser
Status Ready, Watch, Your Turn or Game Over

Best is updated when the game ends. Pressing Restart in the middle of a game does not save it.

6. Playing like a pro

  • Remember positions, not colours. All four pads look the same, so think top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.
  • Say it out loud. Repeat the order in your head as 'TL, BR, TL, TR' while you watch.
  • Chunk the pattern. Group the steps in threes, like a phone number.
  • Focus on the new step. The older steps never change, so you only have to learn one new one each round.
  • Tap steadily. Don't rush. Accuracy matters more than speed.
  • Don't tap early. Taps during the Watch phase do nothing.

7. Common questions

Why do all the pads look the same?This version uses gold pads only. The pattern is about where the flashes happen.
Why isn't my tap working?Taps only count when the Status chip says Your Turn.
Does the pattern stay the same each round? Yes. The earlier steps stay the same, and one new random step is added at the end.
Where is my best score saved?In your browser's local storage. It is lost if you clear site data or switch browser or device.
Is there a time limit?No. You can take as long as you need to tap the next pad.

🔢 2048

Slide numbered tiles around a 4 × 4 board. Matching tiles merge into one bigger tile.

  1. Use the Arrow Keys, or swipe on a phone, to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, and your score goes up by the new number.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move that changes the board.
  4. Keep merging to build the 2048 tile.

Pro tip: Keep your biggest tile in one corner and avoid pressing the opposite direction.

🏓 Pong

The classic paddle duel against the CPU. Keep the ball in play and make the CPU miss.

  1. Press Start. The ball launches in a random direction.
  2. Move your mouse or finger up and down over the board to control your paddle on the left.
  3. If the ball gets past the CPU paddle, you score. If it gets past yours, the CPU scores.
  4. The ball gets 5% faster horizontally every time it hits a paddle.

Pro tip: The CPU paddle never speeds up but the ball does, so long rallies work in your favour.

🧱 Breakout

Bounce a golden orb off your paddle to smash all 40 bricks. You have 3 lives.

  1. Press Start Game and move the paddle with the mouse, your finger, the Arrow Keys or A and D.
  2. Every brick the orb hits breaks and scores 10 points.
  3. If the orb falls past your paddle, you lose a life.
  4. Clear every brick to win.

Pro tip: Hit the orb with the edge of the paddle to change its angle and reach the corners.

🔤 Hangman

Guess the hidden gaming word one letter at a time. You can afford 6 wrong guesses.

  1. Press Start Game to get a word. Each blank is one letter.
  2. Click a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  3. A correct letter fills in every matching blank. A wrong one costs a life.
  4. Reveal the whole word before your 6 lives run out. Your total Wins is saved.

Pro tip: Start with common letters like E and A, then use the pattern of blanks to work out the rest.

⚡ Reaction Test

Test your reflexes. Tap the box the moment it turns gold.

  1. Click the box or press Space to begin. It says Wait for gold.
  2. After a random wait of 1 to 4 seconds, the box turns gold and says CLICK NOW.
  3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time in milliseconds is shown.
  4. After five rounds you get your average. Tapping too early cancels the attempt.

Pro tip: Rest a finger on the Space bar and react to the colour. Don't try to guess the timing.

Play Reaction Test →

💣 Minesweeper

A 9 × 9 board hides 10 mines. Reveal every safe square without hitting one.

  1. Press Start Game, then click any square. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
  3. Right-click, or turn on Flag mode on a phone, to mark a mine.
  4. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win. Your fastest time is saved as Best.

Pro tip: Start in the open area your first click creates, then use the numbers to find squares that must be mines.

Play Minesweeper →

🟡 Flappy Orb

Flap a golden orb through the gaps in the pillars. One touch ends the run.

  1. Press Start Game, or just tap the board.
  2. Press Space or the Up Arrow, click, or tap to flap. Gravity pulls the orb down.
  3. Every pillar you pass scores 1 point.
  4. Touching a pillar, the floor or the ceiling ends the run.

Pro tip: Use small, steady taps instead of big bursts, and aim for the middle of each gap.
